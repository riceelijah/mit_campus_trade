# FRONT PAGE

# Welcome to Campus Trade\!

Campus Trade is a campus-wide trading card game designed to help you meet people, explore MIT, and discover the stories that make campus special.

Every card features student artwork, an excerpt from an interview with an upperclassman, and an Exchange Cost: a conversation starter designed to take you beyond the usual “What’s your name?” and “Where are you from?”

As you meet people, trade cards, and build your collection, you’ll discover new corners of campus and the people who call them home. Log your collection here in the Campus Trade Portal and see what parts of MIT you choose to collect..

Meet people. Trade cards. Start conversations.

Because at the end of the day, the real treasure is the friends you make along the way.

# RULES

# **RULES OF PLAY**

### TL;DR

The goal of Campus Trade is simple: trade cards, talk to people, and complete collection goals.

Campus Trade takes place over one week during Orientation. As the week goes on, you’ll receive new cards, meet new people, discover new objectives, and build your collection.

---

**DAY ONE : START TRADING**

On Monday, every student will receive a Campus Trade Starter Pack containing 6 cards. Your cards will be colored to match your Orientation flag color.

From there, you're on your own.

You are free to move around campus, meet other students, and trade cards. Every card you collect can also be logged in your Campus Trade Portal to keep track of your collection.

**Your Starting Goal: Get one card from every flag color IN HAND**

You may notice that this is IMPOSSIBLE on day one, as you start with 6 cards. This is intentional. This is a *Multi-Day* game, so don’t rush through interactions. Take your time\! Meet someone new\! On day two, you will receive more cards that make this goal possible. 

**Important:** Having a card in your online collection does *not* count. You need to physically have one card from each color in your possession when you verify with the prize booth. 

---

**HOW TO TRADE**

Trading is the heart of Campus Trade.

To complete a trade, the person receiving a card must answer the Exchange Cost on the back of that card.

For example:

> You want my Blue card.  
> The Exchange Cost says: *“What is something you could talk about for hours?”*  
> You answer the question, and the card is yours\!  
> Repeat this process for your trade partner and see where the conversation takes you\!

The questions are designed to get conversations started and help you learn something about the people you're meeting.

There are no wrong answers. Just talk to each other\!

Once you've completed the exchange, make sure to log your new card into the Campus Trade website by scanning the QR Code found on the back. This way you can see all of the cards’ info and add it to your collection.

---

**DAY TWO : THE GAME CHANGES**

On Tuesday, students who return to Orientation events will receive a Day Two Pack.

These packs contain higher-difficulty cards that were not available on Day One. They also introduce Objective Cards, giving you new ways to play and new things to collect.

**Objectives**

Objective Cards give you individual goals to complete throughout the week. These typically revolve around card set completion, indicated by the icons in the upper right of each Campus Trade card. 

Once you've completed an objective, bring your collection to the **Campus Trade Booth** to verify it and earn points for your team\!

Complete objectives to earn individual prizes, including Campus Trade T-shirts, tote bags, and other rewards\!

---

**THE CAMPUS TRADE BOOTH**

The Campus Trade Booth will be open at different points throughout Orientation.

Come by to verify completed objectives, score points for your team, pick up prizes, and talk to the Campus Trade team\! We will have Campus Trade t-shirts, tote bags, buttons, and plenty of other cool prizes to collect. 

And, of course, come say hi. We like talking to people too.

---

**UPPERCLASSMEN**

Keep your eyes open.

Scattered throughout MIT's campus are upperclassmen with special Campus Trade packs. These cards contain rare cards and higher-depth questions that you won't find in regular student packs.

Look for students wearing Campus Trade pins.

If you see one, go introduce yourself. They might have exactly the card you're looking for.

---

**HOW THE GAME ENDS**

At the Orientation Closing Ceremony, we'll announce the final stats from the week \[total \# of trades, most/least popular cards, which team traded the most, etc.\] We'll tally the final scores and announce our winners.

After that, the formal Campus Trade game is over, but your cards are yours\! If you want to keep trading, collecting, or building your collection after Orientation, go for it.

---

**A FINAL NOTE**

Look. We know you're MIT students.

You are going to find loopholes. You're going to test the rules. You're going to discover things we never thought of and you might even break the game entirely. And that's okay.

Campus Trade is a prototype. There are almost certainly oversights, weird edge cases, and things we haven't thought of yet. If you find one, please have a little courtesy when playing.

Ultimately, we can’t force you to talk to anyone. We made a cool game and gave you cards, but the rest is up to you. If you speedrun this game and optimize it for efficiency, or break it for more points, you don’t cheat anyone but yourself. Because the point of Campus Trade isn’t really about the cards, the points, or the prizes. 

It’s about the friends you make along the way. 

# FAQs

# **FAQ’s**

**How does rarity work? Are some cards more scarce than others?**

Each card has a 1–3 star symbol in the corner. This **DOES NOT indicate rarity**. It indicates the depth of the question / exchange cost.

* **Level 1:** Easy, low-stakes questions — *“What was your favorite childhood TV show?”*  
* **Level 2:** A little more personal — *“What is something you’ve always wanted to learn?”*  
* **Level 3:** Deeper questions — *“What is a quality you admire most in others? What qualities do others admire in you?”*

No regular card in Campus Trade was intentionally designed to be more scarce than another. That being said, there may be a few holographic cards floating around… 👀

---

**What exactly does it mean to “collect” a card?**

When you scan a card, you can choose to add it to your collection as either **Collected** or **Seen**.

**Collected** means that you physically have the card and have officially added it to your collection.

**Seen** means that you've encountered the card and want to keep track of it, but you don't currently own it.

Once a card has been added to your collection as Collected, it stays in your online collection even if you later trade the physical card away.

---

**Can I trade a card that I’ve already logged in my collection?**

Yes\! Once you've logged a card in your online collection, you can trade the physical card away. Your online collection will remember that you collected it, even after it changes hands.

We’ll take care of the card’s movement in the backend, so you won’t lose that card from your collection.

---

**How do I actually complete a trade?**

To complete a trade, the person receiving a card must answer the **Exchange Cost** printed on the back of that card.

The questions are designed to get conversations started, not to test you. There are no right or wrong answers, and you don't need to give a perfect or profound response.

Once the question has been answered, the card can change hands and the trade is complete\!

---

**Do I have to answer each question honestly?**

We can't actually *enforce* that any question is answered honestly \[and we're not trying to.\]

Campus Trade works because people choose to have real conversations with each other. We encourage you to answer sincerely, but ultimately, the questions are conversation starters, not interrogations.

If you don't want to answer a particular question, you can always pass on the trade.

---

**Can I negotiate my own trades?**

Sure\! You can negotiate your trades however you'd like. Trade one card for one, trade multiple cards at once, make uneven trades, or work out whatever deal you and the other player agree on.

Just remember: every trade should still include the Exchange Cost for each card being received. More cards exchanged means more conversations\!

---

**What makes the Day Two packs special?**

Day One packs are coded to your Orientation color team. Day Two packs are completely randomized and contain cards that were not available on Day One, including higher-level cards.

If you want to expand your collection, you'll definitely want to pick one up.

---

**How do I complete an Objective, and how do I claim my prize?**

Objective Cards give you additional collection goals beyond the main color collection.

Once you believe you've completed an Objective, bring your collection to the Campus Trade Booth while it is open. We'll verify your collection, award the appropriate points to your team, and let you claim any prize associated with the Objective.

If the booth isn't open yet, don't worry\! Check the schedule for the next opening and keep your cards safe until then.

---

**How are points awarded?**

Points are awarded to your Orientation color team for:

* Each student who completes the main color collection goal  
* Secondary Objectives completed by members of your team

The exact scoring may vary depending on the Objective, but each team should have equal opportunity to earn points, as long as its members participate. 

---

**Can I trade multiple cards at once?**

Sure\! You can negotiate your trades however you'd like. Trade one card for one, trade multiple cards at once, or make whatever deal you and the other player agree on. :\]

---

**Can I trade with someone on my own color team?**

Absolutely\!

In fact, we think this is a great way to get to know your teammates and see what cards they've collected throughout the week.

There's no restriction on who you can trade with. If they have a card you want, go talk to them\!

---

**What happens if I lose a card?**

Unfortunately, we don't have enough cards to replace every lost card.

If you lose one, come by the Campus Trade Booth and ask if we have any extras available. We may be able to help—but don't count on it\!

Take care of your cards. They're collectibles, after all. :\]

---

**Why can't I see every card in the collection?**

This is intentional\!

Go talk to people and trade for cards. The Campus Trade Portal is designed to reward you for actually finding cards rather than simply browsing a database.

You can also scan a card and select “Just Looking” to add it to your collection as Seen without marking it as Collected.

---

**I collected all the cards I need. What do I do now?**

Head to the Campus Trade Booth when it's open to have your collection verified and claim your prize\!

If the booth isn't open yet, check the schedule for the next opening. Or, better yet, keep trading. There are always more cards, more objectives, and more people to meet.

---

**Do I have to participate?**

Nope\!

Campus Trade is here to give you an excuse to meet people, start conversations, and explore MIT. You don't have to participate in any part of the game that you don't want to.

That said, we think you'll have a lot more fun if you give it a shot. **You never know who you'll meet \[or what card you'll find\].**

---

**Can I keep my cards after Orientation?**

**Yes\!**

The formal Campus Trade game ends at the Orientation Closing Ceremony, but the cards you collect are yours to keep.

Want to keep trading with your new friends after Orientation? Go for it. The game may end, but your collection doesn't have to.
